## Context

Analysis of death records to establish public health policy, in a Fiocruz project in Nova Iguaçu.

## What I did

**Shiny dashboard.** Interactive visualization of Rio de Janeiro mortality data for the people who decide public policy.

**Data processing.** Preprocessing, filters and processing of the datasets.

**Machine learning.** Clustering with unsupervised learning to find patterns in the cases.

**Performance.** A caching strategy to reduce the dashboard's response time.
